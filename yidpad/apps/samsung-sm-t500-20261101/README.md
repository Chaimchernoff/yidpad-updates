on Android 12; five require SDK32. Incompatible entries do not prevent the
client from accepting the catalog or downloading compatible selections.

Use build129 or newer for this complete catalog. Build128 was an uninstalled
filtered-catalog candidate; its catalog-wide SDK check is superseded.

catalogs, APK publisher identities, versions, whole/chunk hashes, transport and
rollout seeds are unchanged. Original signature-verified source catalog SHA256:
`194ba61f90bb23ba8c260e3687ca229edd1fb329cc16b1117f35b3ea80769a50`.
