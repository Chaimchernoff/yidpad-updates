# Samsung SM-T500 starter catalog

Catalog v1 is limited to model SM-T500 and the 27 captured apps whose minimum
Android SDK is at most 31 (Android 12). Its dedicated signing key is pinned
only by the Samsung launcher profile. Existing ONN catalogs and signing keys
are unchanged. APK bytes, publisher identities, versions, chunk hashes, rollout
seeds and transport remain those of the signature-verified original catalog.

Source catalog SHA-256:
`194ba61f90bb23ba8c260e3687ca229edd1fb329cc16b1117f35b3ea80769a50`.

Continue this channel with a higher catalog version and the same dedicated
key; do not overwrite its history with a new version 1. Catalog expiry remains
enforced. This metadata does not establish app licensing or hardware acceptance.
