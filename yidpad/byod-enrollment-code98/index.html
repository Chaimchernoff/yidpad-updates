YidPad setup — build 98

At the tablet’s first Welcome screen, tap the same blank spot six times, connect Wi-Fi when asked, then scan.

Finish Android setup and create your private Parent PIN. Home starts empty. Tap + Add your first app, enter your Parent PIN, and choose the apps you want. The parent app list shows icons and descriptions for all 32 starter apps.

Only apps you add download over Wi-Fi. You can return Home while they download. Each selected app shows its progress and becomes available after installation is verified.

Android may ask for installation or security confirmation. App accounts and subscriptions are separate.
