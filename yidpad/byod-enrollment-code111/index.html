YidPad setup · build 111

Version 0.13.68 · ONN model 100135925 · controlled enrollment test

This installs real tablet-management software. Use a new or prepared, supported tablet at Android’s Welcome screen. Resetting an existing tablet erases its local data; follow its preparation guide first.

Tap the same blank spot on the Welcome screen six times. Connect to Wi-Fi when prompted, then scan this QR with Android’s enrollment scanner.

Automatic reset protection

This signed release applies the previously configured central YidPad administrator recovery account during fresh setup. Enrollment cannot complete until Android reports the enabled policy with the exact account set and Google Play services has been notified. No administrator password is included or needed during enrollment.

Finish Android setup, then create your private Parent PIN. If reset protection cannot be confirmed, setup remains incomplete; keep the tablet with the administrator and resolve the reported issue rather than repeatedly resetting it.

Policy readback is not a physical recovery-reset test. Fresh enrollment and post-reset enforcement of this release have not yet been verified on a physical tablet. FRP restricts activation after a reset; it does not reinstall YidPad automatically.

After setup, check Parent Mode → Advanced device information. The expected reset-protection status is POLICY_READBACK_MATCHED_WIPE_UNTESTED. Existing parent-restoration enrollments retain their signed recovery profile.
