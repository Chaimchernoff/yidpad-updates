YIDPAD · SUPERVISED RECOVERY PILOT

Restore your YidPad

Keep this page open on a separate phone or computer. Use the tablet to scan the code.

Test with YidPad support present. This pilot checks whether Android can reinstall and manage YidPad. It does not yet restore your family settings, apps, or reset protection. Keep the tablet with a parent until support verifies protection.

1. Start at the welcome screen

The tablet must be newly reset and at Android’s first welcome screen. If you already reached the home screen, stop and contact support before another reset. A normal app download cannot restore full management.

2. Scan during setup

  1. Tap the same blank spot on the tablet’s welcome screen six times.
  2. Follow Android’s prompts to connect Wi-Fi and open the QR scanner.
  3. Scan the code below and follow the management setup prompts.

Scan with the tablet’s setup scanner.

If Google asks to verify an account

Stop and contact YidPad support. Support must coordinate authorized verification. Never share a Google password or verification code, and do not add the shared support account to the tablet for everyday use.

If Android blocks the installation

If you see “App blocked to protect your device,” stop and report that message. Support must resolve Android’s management-app approval requirement. Do not disable security checks or keep resetting the tablet.

3. Finish with support

Support must verify YidPad management, set up your private Parent PIN, reapply reset protection, reconnect the parent portal, restore approved apps, and confirm locked Child Mode. Scanning this code alone does not complete recovery.

This code contains a YidPad download address and checksum. It does not contain a Google login, Parent PIN, or permission to claim an existing family tablet.
